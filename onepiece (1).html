Akuma no Mi
Devil Fruits
Paramecia
Body-altering superhuman abilities. Most common type. Examples: Gum-Gum, String-String, Flower-Flower.
Zoan
Animal transformations granting their traits. Mythical Zoans are rarest and most powerful.
Logia
Become, generate, and control a natural element. User is near-untouchable without Haki.
⚠️ Weakness
All Devil Fruit users lose the ability to swim. Sea water drains their power. Seastone negates abilities.
👁️
Busoshoku · Kenbunshoku · Haoshoku
Haki
Observation Haki (Kenbunshoku)
Sense presence, emotions, and predict movements. Advanced users see the future.
Armament Haki (Busoshoku)
Harden the body or weapons. Only way to bypass Logia defenses. Advanced form flows through targets.
Conqueror's Haki (Haoshoku)
Rare willpower that subdues weak-willed enemies. Advanced coating multiplies physical power tenfold.

Fighting Arts
Rokushiki (Six Powers)
CP9 martial arts: Geppo (sky-walk), Soru (speed), Tekkai (iron body), Rankyaku (wind blades), Shigan (finger pistol), Kami-e (paper dodge).
Fish-Man Karate
Martial art using water molecules in the air. Works at range. Devastating against large enemies.
Ryusoken / Electro / Black Leg
Dragon Claw, Mink electricity manipulation, and Sanji's flame-kick style. Each unique to bloodline or training.
